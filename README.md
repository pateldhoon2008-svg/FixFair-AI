# ⚖️ FixFair AI

> Evidence-driven AI for fair dispute resolution.

## 🚨 Problem

Online disputes are often difficult to resolve because users have
scattered evidence, complicated policies, and unclear negotiation options.

People may know that something went wrong, but they often do not know:

- What facts actually matter
- What evidence supports their claim
- Which policy applies
- What would be a reasonable resolution
- How to communicate with the other party

## 💡 Solution

FixFair AI is an AI-powered dispute resolution assistant that helps
users structure their dispute, organize evidence, understand the issue,
and generate fair possible resolutions.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🔍 **Dispute Analysis** — Converts a messy complaint into structured facts.
- 📎 **Evidence Vault** — Allows users to upload supporting evidence.
- 📜 **Policy Awareness** — Supports relevant policy documents.
- ⚖️ **Fair Resolution** — Suggests multiple reasonable outcomes.
- 💬 **Negotiation Support** — Generates a professional negotiation message.
- 🛡️ **Verification Pipeline** — Designed to separate evidence,
  policy reasoning, resolution, and verification.

## 🔄 How It Works

User's Dispute
↓
Evidence Upload
↓
Case Structuring
↓
AI Analysis
↓
Fair Resolution
↓
Negotiation Support
